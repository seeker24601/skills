# Research

Use for a research question, disputed claim, or factual briefing.

1. Establish the exact question, relevant period, authorized sources, and needed confidence. Ordinary conversation does not require research.
2. Load `research-and-fact-check` to gather and verify decisive evidence. Split independent questions only when parallel research would save time; give workers bounded source scopes and require inspected sources and gaps. Reconcile evidence yourself rather than counting repeated citations as confirmation.
3. If interpretation requires a specialist method, load `business-analysis` or `people-and-incentives` only for that part. Use `workflows/evaluate.md` when the user needs a choice or forecast.
4. Stop when evidence answers the question or the decisive gap is clear. Lead with the supported answer, cite sources, distinguish inference, and state important uncertainty. Save a report only when requested or part of the task.
