# Curate

Use for organizing, maintaining, or auditing a bounded part of the vault. Follow the preservation and tool contract in `AGENTS.md`; load `record-keeping` for note conventions and saving.

1. Establish the folder, project, or records in scope. Read its index and conventions. Use existing Home, Inbox, and folder indexes; introduce folders only when they aid retrieval. Scheduled maintenance requires a user-requested schedule.
2. Inspect pending inbox items, broken links, likely duplicates, stale claims, and unlinked notes. Read candidate records before deciding their disposition. Age alone does not invalidate a claim; an unlinked private draft may be intentional.
3. Load `dissolve-page` for consolidation, redistribution, or retirement. Map unique content to canonical owners, write and verify destinations first, then repair links and retain a source pointer. The bundled tool cannot delete or rename.
4. Load `prompt-token-trimmer` for repetitive synthesized or LLM-facing notes. Preserve raw sources, historical records, protected material, and user-facing prose. Prefer linking to the canonical explanation over repeating it. Split a synthesized page only when its subjects have distinct owners and the restructuring is authorized.
5. Resolve stale claims only when evidence settles them; otherwise retain uncertainty and conflicting accounts. Mark superseded decisions as history with a replacement link.
6. Make scoped repairs using fresh content hashes. Read changed notes back; verify links, indexes, and stale references. Explain partial failures and remaining coverage limits.

Use meaningful `[[Folder/Note]]` links with full paths when filenames repeat. Graph proximity is layout, not evidence of a relationship. Index or graph limits may hide records; use scoped searches and never claim a complete audit without complete coverage.

Report changed pages, verified repairs, unresolved conflicts, and pending cleanup. Record a substantial restructuring where useful; avoid a new maintenance log for every minor edit.
