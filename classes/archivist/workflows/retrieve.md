# Retrieve

Use when asked to find records, recall a documented decision, or synthesize existing knowledge.

1. Establish the question and authorized vault scope. Identify subjects, aliases, likely titles, and dates.
2. Load `evidence-retrieval` to search, read candidate pages, follow relevant links, and assess their authority. Narrow queries when results are truncated.
3. Answer from the evidence with page references. Distinguish source facts, inference, conflicting accounts, and missing coverage. Do not convert an unsuccessful search into a claim that an event never happened.
4. Keep retrieval read-only unless the user also requests a saved answer or record correction. Use `workflows/archive.md` for an authorized saved synthesis and `workflows/curate.md` for scoped maintenance.

Lead with the answer and include only the evidence and limitations needed to assess it. Do not silently broaden the search into another companion's records or external sources.
