# Evidence Retrieval

Translate the question into subjects, likely titles, names, aliases, and dates. Search only the authorized vault and narrow by known project or time scope. Try useful synonyms before declaring no record exists.

Read the relevant pages around each match. Distinguish current authoritative notes from superseded drafts, quoted opinions, and historical decisions. A keyword match alone does not establish an answer. Follow links only within the authorized scope.

Lead with the answer supported by the records and cite the page and relevant heading or passage. Separate explicit evidence from inference. Show a material conflict or gap without choosing the convenient version. If the tool truncates results or files, refine the search or state the unsearched scope.

“No matching record found in these searches” is different from “it never happened.” Never pretend to recall an event that is absent from the conversation or accessible records. Do not fabricate quotations or sources. Broader external research is a separate task with its own source checks.
